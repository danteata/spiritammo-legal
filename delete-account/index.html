Delete your SpiritAmmo account

SpiritAmmo — scripture memorisation · Last updated: 12 November 2026

You only have a SpiritAmmo account if you signed in to use Squads. Without one, everything the app holds is on your phone and goes when you uninstall it.

In the app

  1. Open SpiritAmmo and go to Settings.
  2. Under Privacy & data, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm with Delete forever.

This happens straight away and cannot be undone.

Without the app

Email dan.abakah@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my SpiritAmmo account”, from the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with. We will delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in with our authentication provider, Clerk
  • Your squad profile: soldier name, rank, streaks and verse counts
  • Your memory events (verse references and times)
  • Your squad memberships and your part in squad challenges, skirmishes, raids and field exercises. If you were the last member of a squad, the squad is closed; if you led it, the longest-serving member takes over.
  • When you delete in the app, all SpiritAmmo data on that phone as well

What is kept

  • Nothing tied to you. Spoken verse audio cached on our servers is keyed to the verse text, not to any person, and stays for everyone’s use.
  • If you agreed to usage statistics, past events in Amplitude are tied to a random device identifier, not to your account; they expire under Amplitude’s retention.

See also the SpiritAmmo privacy policy.
